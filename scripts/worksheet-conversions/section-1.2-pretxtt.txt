\end{axis}
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{minipage}
</p>
</solution>

<introduction>
<p>
The total cost of a meal in a restaurant, <m>C</m>, in dollars, as a function of the price of the meal, <m>p</m>, in dollars is given by:
<me>
C=p+0.20p
</me>
where the term <m>0.20p</m> corresponds to the 20\% tip. Create a table of values and sketch the graph of the function <m>C=p+0.20p</m> for <m>10\leq p\leq 20</m>. Then use your calculator or any other graphing utility to check your graph. 

\begin{solution}[16em]
We can combine like terms and write <m>C = 1.20 p</m>. We then have the following.

\begin{minipage}{0.4\textwidth}
\centering
\begin{tabular}{c|c}
$p$ & $C=1.20p$ \\\hline
$10$ & $12$\\\hline
$15$ & $18$\\\hline
$20$ & $24$
\end{tabular} 
\end{minipage} \hfill
\begin{minipage}{0.6\textwidth}
\centering
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.8]
\begin{axis}[grid=both,
axis lines=middle,
xmin=0,xmax=20,
ymin=0,ymax=25,
xtick={5,10,15,20},
ytick={5,10,15,20,25},
minor tick={},
xlabel=\(p\), ylabel=\(C\),
samples=250]
\addplot[domain=0:20,thick] {1.2*x};
\end{axis}
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{minipage} 
\end{solution}

<exercise>
<introduction>
<p>
The amount of caffeine remaining in the body, <m>C</m>, in milligrams, <m>t</m> hours after drinking a cup of coffee, <m>C=C(t)</m> is given by the graph below:

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.8]
\begin{axis}[grid=both,
axis lines=middle,
axis line style = very thick,
xmin=0,xmax=20,
ymin=0,ymax=96,
xtick={5,10,15,20},
ytick={24,48,72,96},
minor tick={},
xlabel=\(t\), ylabel=\(C\),
samples=250]
\addplot[domain=0:20,thick] {96*0.5^(x/5)};
\end{axis}
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}
</p>
</introduction>

<task workspace="1in">
<statement>
<p>
How much caffeine was absorbed into the bloodstream from the cup of coffee?
</p>
</statement>
<solution>
<p>
This is the value at <m>t=0</m> hours, so <m>96</m> milligrams.
</p>
</solution>
</task>


<task workspace="1in">
<statement>
<p>
How much caffeine is left after 5 hours? After 10 hours?
</p>
</statement>
<solution>
<p>
After <m>5</m> hours, there are <m>C(5)=48</m> mg of caffeine remaining. After <m>10</m> hours, there are <m>C(10)=24</m> mg of caffeine remaining.
</p>
</solution>
</task>



<task workspace="1in">
<statement>
<p>
Is the function <m>C(t)</m> increasing, decreasing or neither on the interval <m>t\geq 0</m>?
</p>
</statement>
<solution>
<p>
The function is decreasing on <m>t\ge 0</m>; the outputs decrease as the inputs increase.
</p>
</solution>
</task>

<statement>
<p>
For the function <m>y=f(t)</m> whose graph is depicted below, identify intervals on the <m>t</m>-axis on which the function is increasing and the intervals on the <m>t</m>-axis on which the function is decreasing.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.9]
\begin{axis}[grid=both,
axis lines=middle,
axis line style = very thick,
xmin=0,xmax=13,
ymin=-2,ymax=4,
xtick={1,...,12},
ytick={-1,1,2,3},
minor tick={},
xlabel=\(t\), ylabel=\(y\),
trig format = rad,
samples=250]
\addplot[domain=0:12,thick] {2*sin(2*3.14/12*x)+1};
\end{axis}
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}
</p>
</statement>
<solution>
<p>
The function is increasing on the intervals <m>(0,3)</m> and <m>(9,12)</m>, which in inequality notation reads <m>0<t<3</m> and <m>9<t<12</m>, respectively.

The function is decreasing on the interval <m>(3,9)</m>, which in inequality notation reads <m>3<t<9</m>.
</p>
</solution>
