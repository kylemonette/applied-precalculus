\question A man takes $3$ seconds to breathe in and out once. The volume of air in his lungs, $V(t)$, varies between a minimum of $0.5$ liters and a maximum of $4.5$ liters. Assume that at time $t=0$ the volume is at a minimum; that is, time $t=0$ is when the man begins to breathe in.

\begin{parts}
\part What are the period, amplitude, and midline of $V(t)$?

\begin{solution}[10em]
The period is $3$ seconds. The amplitude is
\[
\text{Amp. } \quad=\quad \frac{y_\text{max} -y_\text{min} }{2} \quad = \quad  \frac{4.5-0.5}{2}  \quad = \quad 2 \text{ liters}
.\] The midline equation is
\[
y \quad = \quad \frac{y_\text{max}  + y_\text{min} }{2}  \quad = \quad \frac{4.5+0.5}{2}  \quad = \quad 2.5
.\] 
\end{solution}


\part Sketch a possible graph of $V(t)$ which shows two periods of $V(t)$.

\begin{solution}[16em]

\end{solution}


\end{parts}

\question Estimate the period of the following periodic function $f(t)$:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|} \hline
$t$ & $0$ & $1$ & $2$ & $3$ & $4$ & $5$ & $6$ \\\hline
$f(t)$ & $12$ & $13$ & $14$ & $12$ & $13$ & $14$ & $12$ \\\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{solution}[7em]
The outputs $12$, $13$, $14$ are repeating in this pattern. And, it takes $3$ units of $x$ to do so, therefore the period of this function is $3$.
\end{solution}


\question Estimate the period of the following periodic function $g(x)$:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|} \hline
$x$ & $1$ & $11$ & $21$ & $31$ & $41$ & $51$ & $61$ & $71$ & $81$ \\\hline
$g(x)$ & $5$ & $3$ & $2$ & $3$ & $5$ & $3$ & $2$ & $3$ & $5$ \\\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{solution}
The outputs $5$, $3$, $2$, $3$ are repeating in this pattern. And, it takes $40$ units of $x$ to do so, therefore the period is $40$.
\end{solution}

Graphs (A) and (B) are periodic meanwhile (C) and (D) are not.

\end{solution}
