    Introduction

    Describing the path of a projectile, designing lenses to bend and focus light, and maximizing revenue in certain business situations are just a few of the places that quadratic functions can be utilized. With “U”-shaped graphs known as parabolas, these functions allow us to model and understand a remarkable array of real-world phenomena.
